# Pumpit Raw: one-knob sidechain pump & glue

![Pumpit Raw free one-knob sidechain pump UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/PumpitRaw/main/pumpitraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**Glue your kick and sub together with a single knob.**

Pumpit Raw is the free, one-knob version of PumpIt. Turn up the **Glue** knob and a tempo-synced sidechain pump locks your low end together: punchy, auto-leveled, and ready on any track with no routing.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/PumpitRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`PumpitRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Pumpit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`PumpitRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Pumpit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **Glue** knob. Turn it up and a tempo-synced sidechain envelope ducks your track in time with the beat, gluing kick and sub together with punchy, auto-leveled ducking. No sidechain routing to set up. Drop it on a track and dial in the pump.

**Windows**

- A VST3 or CLAP host
