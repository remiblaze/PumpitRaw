# Pumpit Raw — one-knob sidechain pump & glue

![Pumpit Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/PumpitRaw/main/pumpitraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**Glue your kick and sub together with a single knob.**

Pumpit Raw is the free, one-knob version of PumpIt. Turn up the **Glue** knob and a tempo-synced sidechain pump locks your low end together — punchy, auto-leveled, and ready on any track with no routing.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/PumpitRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`PumpitRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **Glue** knob. Turn it up and a tempo-synced sidechain envelope ducks your track in time with the beat, gluing kick and sub together with punchy, auto-leveled ducking. No sidechain routing to set up — drop it on a track and dial in the pump.
